PRISM SHIFT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how PRISM SHIFT (the "App"), published by techsnort ("we"), handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not create accounts and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information ourselves. Your game progress (level, Prism Shards, skins, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows rewarded video ads and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to personalize ads where permitted. In the European Economic Area, UK and similar regions we ask for your consent using Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the App's privacy options.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

The optional "Ad-Free Pack" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play shares purchase status with the App so we can unlock your purchase and restore it on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because game data lives only on your device, uninstalling the App removes it. Data held by Google (AdMob, Google Play) is governed by Google's policies.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
